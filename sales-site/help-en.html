①Getting started

  • Register at kp-sales.web.app with e-mail + password
  • Wait for KP approval — then you see prices, stock and can order
  • Profile (top right): enter your commission bank account and choose ค่ะ/ครับ
  • Tap 🔔 Turn on notifications to know instantly when KP replies or confirms an order
  • Forgot password → tap Forgot password? and check your e-mail (also the Spam folder)

②Products view only

  • Search by code, colour or number · filter WPC / PVC / trims / clips / in stock
  • Stock shows the quantity you can sell · Temporarily sold out = cannot be sold yet
  • 🧮 Panel calculator: width × height or enter m² (waste already included)
  • Share to Facebook / LINE (photo + text) · copy text · save photo
  • See the matching trims (L / H / U) for each model

③Quotation

  • 📋 Paste the customer's message → the app fills in name, phone, address, province
  • + Add product: search, tap = +1 · +L / +H buttons under the panel · calculator per line
  • Send to customer: as an image or as text
  • Save & wait for customer → stays in the 📁 folder, searchable by name, phone, LINE, FB
  • Customer doesn't buy → tap Not buying · reorder from the folder

④Send the order to KP

  • Customer agrees → open the quote from the folder, no need to type it again
  • Required: name · 10-digit phone · address · province (+ Google Maps pin if available)
  • Tap Send to KP → check the summary → Confirm
  • You get your own order number immediately, e.g. NOK-0001
  • KP checks it and gives the KP order number (#T-…) — notification to your phone

⑤Customer payment

  • Cash on delivery: customer pays the delivery staff
  • Pay in advance: transfer to the KP company account only
  • Attach the slip when sending the order, or later on the order page
  • KP checks the slip → Slip OK ✓ or Rejected + reason
  • ❌ Never take customer money into a personal account

⑥Order tracking

  • Status: Waiting for KP → Confirmed → Packing → On delivery → Delivered → Paid
  • While on delivery you see the driver's name or the shipping company
  • Search by name, phone, order no. · 9 filters, e.g. Unpaid
  • Copy status and send it to the customer right away
  • Damaged goods → Report a claim with photos within 7 days

⑦Earnings / commission

  • Counts when the customer has paid in full, by the payment date (not the order date) · deposits don't count yet
  • Per order: bill → base × % = commission (base = goods, without shipping)
  • Closed at month end · KP transfers on the 1st of the next month
  • Create your commission invoice (PDF) from the 1st
  • See what KP has already paid on the same page

⑧Sales kit & KP chat

  • 💡 Sales kit (top button): KP rules · ready-made answers · handling hesitant customers
  • Write and save your own answers, copy and reuse them
  • 💬 KP chat (round button, bottom right): ask anytime
  • Point at the screen or attach a photo so KP sees exactly what you mean
  • Reply within ~15 minutes during office hours (daily 08:00–18:00)

!Important rules read before selling

  • Shipping: 25 panels or more free, below that 400 ฿ · islands (ferry) +400 ฿/box
  • Clips: 10 clip-locks free per panel
  • Discounts: at most your own commission (deducted from it) · never post discounted prices on your page
  • 100 panels or more: ask the office for a special price
  • You may say: waterproof · termite-proof · fire-retardant · for walls and ceilings
  • Never promise: 100% fireproof · outdoor use · X-year warranty
  • KP offers no installation and gives no installation advice
  • Returns only for damaged goods · calculate the quantity correctly before ordering
  • Customer refuses delivery: no commission, and you help pay the return shipping

❌ Never

  • Let the customer transfer to your own account
  • Send fake slips / wrong amounts
  • Collect cash instead of KP

11Important rules — do / don't

Make sure you understand them before selling · the full version is in Sales kit → KP rules

✅ Allowed / good to know

  • Shipping: 25 panels or more ship free · below that 400 ฿ · L-Corner only: ≤10 pieces 250 ฿, 11+ pieces 400 ฿
  • Islands (ferry) +400 ฿/box · other provinces via carrier, cash on delivery possible
  • Free clip-locks: 10 per panel
  • Discounts: at most your own commission (deducted from it)
  • 100 panels or more: ask the office for a special price
  • You may say: waterproof · termite-proof · fire-retardant · for walls and ceilings
  • Colour samples: free pick-up at the shop · delivered 100 ฿ · all models 350 ฿
  • Customer wants to see the goods at the showroom — tell KP in the chat first

❌ Not allowed

  • Taking customer money into a personal account
  • Promising 100% fireproof · outdoor use · X-year warranty · "we manufacture it ourselves"
  • Posting discounted prices on your page
  • Commenting on or poaching customers on KP's page
  • Promising installation — KP offers no installation and gives no installation advice
  • Selling items marked temporarily sold out without asking KP

⚠ Consequences

  • Customer refuses delivery: no commission + you help pay the return shipping · after 2 times, your next customer must pay a deposit first
  • Exaggerated claims lead to a complaint: you return the commission for that order + share responsibility for the damage
  • Returns only for damaged goods — over-ordering / change of mind is not taken back
